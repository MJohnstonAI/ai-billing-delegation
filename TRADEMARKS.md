# ABDS Project Name and Trademark Policy

This policy covers use of the project names **ABDS** and **Artificial Intelligence Billing Delegation Standard**, associated NeuroSync AI Dynamics branding, and any future ABDS logos or certification marks.

This document does not claim that every listed name or mark is registered in every jurisdiction. It states the project's usage and endorsement policy and preserves rights that may exist under applicable trademark, passing-off, unfair-competition, or related law.

## Permitted descriptive use

You may make truthful descriptive references such as:

- "implements concepts from the ABDS v0.7 draft";
- "experimental implementation of the ABDS Provider Adoption Profile";
- "based on the public Artificial Intelligence Billing Delegation Standard proposal"; or
- "compatible with selected ABDS draft interfaces"

provided the statement is accurate and does not imply certification, sponsorship, endorsement, or Provider adoption.

## Restricted claims

Without prior written authorization from NeuroSync AI Dynamics (Pty) Ltd, do not use names or branding in a way that reasonably implies an official relationship, including claims such as:

```text
ABDS Certified
Official ABDS
ABDS Approved
NeuroSync Approved
ABDS Partner
ABDS Foundation
ABDS Consortium
```

unless that status has actually been granted under a documented program.

Do not use the project name or branding to suggest that Google, OpenAI, Anthropic, OpenRouter, or another Provider has adopted or endorsed ABDS unless that Provider has publicly or contractually confirmed the claim.

## Product and company names

A third party should not name a competing commercial product, service, company, certification program, or domain in a way that is likely to create confusion with the ABDS project or NeuroSync AI Dynamics' stewardship.

Fair, nominative, comparative, journalistic, academic, and critical references remain subject to applicable law and are not intended to be prohibited by this policy.

## Forks and modified specifications

Forks and adaptations permitted under an applicable licence must clearly identify material changes and must not present themselves as the official NeuroSync-maintained ABDS specification unless authorized.

A modified fork should use wording such as:

> "Based on ABDS v0.7; modified independently and not endorsed by NeuroSync AI Dynamics."

## Future certification

If a formal conformance or certification program is established, the terms for using certification marks will be published separately. Passing repository tests alone does not confer certification.

## Contact

Requests for official partnership, certification, branding, commercial licensing, or co-marketing should be directed to NeuroSync AI Dynamics (Pty) Ltd through the project repository or the company's published contact channels.
