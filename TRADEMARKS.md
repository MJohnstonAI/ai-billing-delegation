# ABDS Project Name, Trademark, and False-Origin Policy

This policy covers use of the project names **ABDS** and **Artificial Intelligence Billing Delegation Standard**, associated NeuroSync AI Dynamics branding, and any current or future ABDS logos, badges, certification marks, domain names, or source identifiers.

This document does not claim that every listed name or mark is registered in every jurisdiction. It records the project's usage and endorsement policy and preserves rights that may exist under applicable trade mark, passing-off, unfair-competition, false-designation, company-name, domain-name, or related law.

## 1. Ownership and stewardship position

ABDS was originated by Marc Johnston and is currently stewarded by NeuroSync AI Dynamics (Pty) Ltd.

Copyright, evaluation, source-code, or documentation licences do **not** by themselves grant a trade mark licence or the right to present another organization as the originator, owner, official steward, certifier, or exclusive commercial source of ABDS.

## 2. Permitted descriptive use

Truthful descriptive references are generally acceptable, subject to applicable law and the relevant copyright/software licence, for example:

- "implements concepts from the ABDS v0.7 draft";
- "experimental implementation of the ABDS Provider Adoption Profile";
- "based on the public Artificial Intelligence Billing Delegation Standard proposal";
- "compatible with selected ABDS draft interfaces"; or
- "internal evaluation based on ABDS v0.7.1 materials."

A descriptive use should be no more prominent than reasonably necessary to identify the project and should not imply certification, sponsorship, endorsement, ownership, Provider adoption, or an official relationship that does not exist.

## 3. Restricted source-identifier uses

Without prior written authorization from NeuroSync AI Dynamics (Pty) Ltd, do not use **ABDS**, **Artificial Intelligence Billing Delegation Standard**, or confusingly similar branding as or as a dominant part of:

- a company or trading name;
- a competing product or commercial service name;
- a hosted platform or API name;
- a certification or conformance business;
- a standards foundation, consortium, association, or governance body;
- a domain name or subdomain intended to create source confusion;
- a social-media account or developer-organization identity intended to appear official;
- an app-store listing or commercial package name that implies official ABDS status; or
- a badge, seal, mark, or logo that could reasonably be understood as an ABDS certification or endorsement mark.

## 4. Restricted claims

Without prior written authorization, do not make claims that reasonably imply an official status, including:

```text
ABDS Certified
Official ABDS
ABDS Approved
NeuroSync Approved
ABDS Partner
ABDS Foundation
ABDS Consortium
ABDS Standards Authority
Official ABDS Provider
```

unless that status has actually been granted under a documented program.

Do not use project names or branding to suggest that Google, OpenAI, Anthropic, OpenRouter, or another Provider has adopted, certified, funded, partnered with, or endorsed ABDS unless that Provider has publicly or contractually confirmed the claim.

## 5. False origin and rebranding

A person may not use ABDS branding or protected copied ABDS materials in a way that falsely suggests that person or organization originated the ABDS project or is the owner of copied NeuroSync/ABDS materials.

Where a modified implementation or specification lawfully uses protected ABDS materials, the presentation must comply with the applicable licence's attribution requirements and must not erase or falsify provenance.

Where a third party has independently developed technology without copying protected ABDS materials, this policy does not convert the underlying abstract idea or method into a trade mark or copyright monopoly. However, the third party must still avoid confusing use of ABDS source identifiers and false claims of affiliation or origin.

## 6. Forks and modified specifications

Forks and adaptations permitted under an applicable licence must clearly identify material changes and must not present themselves as the official NeuroSync-maintained ABDS specification unless authorized.

A modified fork should use wording such as:

> "Based on ABDS v0.7; modified independently and not endorsed by NeuroSync AI Dynamics."

Where confusion is likely, a fork or derivative commercial product should use a distinct product name and use **ABDS** only descriptively.

## 7. Provider evaluation

An organization evaluating ABDS under `PROVIDER_EVALUATION_LICENSE.md` may use the ABDS name internally as reasonably necessary to identify the evaluated project.

The evaluation licence does not authorize public co-branding, marketing, certification claims, commercial product naming, or an implication that the organization has acquired the ABDS brand or standard.

## 8. Domains and online identities

Registering a domain, repository organization, package namespace, social-media handle, or online identity containing ABDS terminology does not create ownership of the ABDS project or authority to act for NeuroSync AI Dynamics.

NeuroSync reserves the right to object, where applicable law permits, to registrations or uses that are misleading, confusingly similar, made in bad faith, or likely to divert users seeking the official ABDS project.

## 9. Future registration and certification

NeuroSync AI Dynamics may seek trade mark, collective-mark, or certification-mark protection in one or more jurisdictions.

If a formal conformance or certification program is established, separate rules will govern use of certification names, badges, and marks. Passing repository tests or implementing the draft specification alone does not confer certification.

## 10. No waiver

Failure to object immediately to a third-party use does not intentionally waive rights that may exist under applicable law.

Any express trade mark licence, co-branding right, certification right, or official-partner status should be documented in writing and may include quality-control, territory, duration, revocation, and use requirements.

## 11. Fair and lawful references

Nothing in this policy is intended to prohibit fair, nominative, comparative, journalistic, academic, critical, or other lawful references that do not require permission.
