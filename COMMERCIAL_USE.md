# ABDS Commercial Use and Partnership Policy

ABDS is intended to be reviewable, testable, and useful without giving away every future commercial option of NeuroSync AI Dynamics (Pty) Ltd.

This document explains the commercial path. `LICENSE.md` and the referenced standard licences control actual permissions.

## Public-use model

### Specification and documentation

ABDS v0.7-and-later specification and documentation materials are generally made available under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 unless a file says otherwise.

That public licence supports non-commercial study, review, teaching, research, prototyping, critique, and adaptation subject to its terms. It does not grant commercial use.

### Software and executable tooling

ABDS v0.7-and-later software is generally made available under PolyForm Small Business License 1.0.0 unless a file says otherwise.

Qualifying small businesses may use the software for permitted purposes under that licence. Larger organizations and other uses outside that licence require separate permission.

## Commercial organizations

NeuroSync AI Dynamics welcomes commercial adoption where it is mutually beneficial.

A commercial organization that needs rights beyond the applicable public licence should contact NeuroSync before deploying, incorporating, redistributing, selling, offering as a service, or otherwise exploiting the relevant ABDS material commercially.

Potential arrangements may include:

- a commercial licence;
- a provider design partnership;
- paid implementation or integration work;
- support and maintenance;
- architecture and security review;
- sponsorship of the public standard;
- conformance tooling or certification services if established later;
- strategic investment;
- revenue-, usage-, or milestone-linked consideration; or
- another negotiated agreement.

The appropriate structure depends on the implementation and the value exchanged. ABDS does not impose a public percentage-of-profit formula because such a formula can be ambiguous, difficult to administer, and counterproductive to provider adoption.

## Provider design partners

AI Providers, model routers, cloud platforms, identity platforms, billing platforms, and developer platforms are invited to engage as design partners.

A design partnership may focus on:

- Provider-authoritative entitlement resolution;
- OAuth and consent architecture;
- delegated grant semantics;
- provider-side metering and accounting;
- user-visible limits and revocation;
- sponsored or organization-funded use;
- streaming, multimodal, batch, or agentic settlement;
- threat modeling and abuse prevention;
- interoperability testing; and
- reference implementation work.

A Provider's review of ABDS does not automatically grant NeuroSync access to that Provider's systems, and it does not imply that the Provider has adopted ABDS. Likewise, public availability of the proposal does not create a commitment by NeuroSync to perform Provider-specific engineering without an agreed scope.

## Sponsorship and voluntary support

Organizations and individuals may voluntarily support ABDS research and maintenance through sponsorship, grants, donations, engineering contributions, cloud credits, or other agreed assistance.

Voluntary support does not by itself confer certification, exclusivity, ownership of the standard, or special implementation rights unless a separate written agreement says so.

## No implied endorsement or certification

No organization may describe itself or a product as **ABDS Certified**, **Official ABDS**, **ABDS Approved**, or endorsed by NeuroSync merely because it implements concepts from the repository.

No implementation may claim a named AI Provider has adopted ABDS without evidence from that Provider.

See `TRADEMARKS.md`.

## Historical versions

Earlier ABDS versions were publicly described as MIT licensed. The v0.7 licensing framework does not attempt to revoke rights already validly granted in historical material.

Commercial parties wishing to rely on a historical version should identify the exact historical commit and applicable terms. Rights to v0.7-and-later additions and modifications are governed by the current licensing framework unless a file says otherwise.
